Baby's first HTML5 <video> element

<baby-video> is a from-scratch re-implementation of the <video> element, built on WebCodecs, Web Audio and a hand-written MediaSource. It's here to show how video playback works inside a browser, including playing video backwards.

Source on GitHub Demuxed 2022: Baby's first HTML5 video element Demuxed 2023: The curious player of Benjamin Button

Buffer

The demo keeps 30 seconds buffered in the direction of playback and 10 seconds behind. Switch direction and watch the window flip.
